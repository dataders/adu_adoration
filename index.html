03 / The decisions that matter

A better plan starts
with the tradeoffs.

The original sketch asked for both an office and a substantial sunroom. The current scheme prioritizes the garage. That choice deserves a direct comparison.

Current basis · Option F

Keep the full-depth garage.

A 23-ft clear parking zone keeps the garage useful. The approximately 70-sq-ft garden office gives the ground floor a separate place to work and a door to the yard.

The tradeoff The garden office is not the generous sunroom shown in the original sketch.

See the current ground floor ↗
Alternative · for discussion

Give more room to the garden.

An 18-ft clear parking zone makes room for approximately 121 sq ft of connected garden space. Test this against the actual vehicle and workshop needs.

The tradeoff Reduced parking depth. This is an alternative study, not the selected model.

A building in its context

Leave room
for the yard.

The working site plan places the ADU at the back of the lot, with a south stair, east patios, and a slimmer replacement shed.

Setbacks, grades, drainage, utilities, and access need survey and professional confirmation. Four-foot patios are circulation space first; seating needs a separate fit test.
