Keep

Resolve

Stair, roof within 20 ft height, garage-office fit, windows, grading, drainage, and utilities.

Open setback question

Keep, move, rebuild, or remove? Owner measurement is 12 × 18 ft; assessor record says 18 × 20 ft. Verify before design development.

Design direction

Simple, warm, and compatible with the 1931 house.

Olive or muted green lap siding, a legible roof, generous eaves, divided-light windows, warm accents, and a carriage-style garage door. Craftsman influence without imitation.
